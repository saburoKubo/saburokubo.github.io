FocusDuck Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled in the Android app FocusDuck (application ID: com.kubosaburo.focusduck; referred to below as “the App”).

1. About the App

FocusDuck is a focus-support app where users record focused time while enjoying the growth of a little duck and its pond. The App currently has no proprietary account registration system and no login or synchronization service operated on the developer’s own servers.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally to provide its features and retain its state:

  • Focus duration, number of sessions, and focus records by time period and day
  • Breadcrumbs, duck and pond growth, and pond memories
  • Creatures recorded in the creature book and pond decorations obtained or placed
  • The optional name given to the duck
  • Settings such as focus presets, rest timer, focus sounds, volume, season, and daily goals
  • Status used to manage onboarding, in-app guidance, and the frequency of ad display

The App does not include a feature that sends this data to a server independently operated by the FocusDuck developer. Deleting the App’s data or uninstalling the App deletes the data stored locally on the device.

If Android backup is enabled on the device, some local data may be included in an operating-system backup or restoration. Such processing is governed by the device settings and Google’s services.

3. Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve banner and interstitial ads. Ads are not shown to users with an active FocusDuck Plus entitlement.

Depending on the user’s consent choices, region, device settings, Google’s specifications, and applicable law, Google and parties involved in ad delivery may process information such as IP addresses, online identifiers including advertising identifiers, device information, diagnostic information, and information about ad impressions and interactions. This processing may be used for ad delivery, frequency management, measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

The FocusDuck developer does not use information processed through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to build an independent user profile or to collect names, postal addresses, or email addresses.

4. Advertising Consent Management

The App uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where Google determines that consent or privacy choices must be presented, including certain circumstances in the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App displays an advertising choices form and Google’s systems manage the user’s selections.

When Google determines that privacy options must remain available, users can review or change their selections through “Privacy settings” in the App’s settings screen.

5. FocusDuck Plus and Payments

The App uses Google Play Billing for the one-time FocusDuck Plus product, which provides an ad-free experience and additional features. Purchase processing, purchase status checks, restoration, and required acknowledgment are handled through Google Play.

The FocusDuck developer does not directly obtain payment information such as credit card numbers or bank account details. The App checks the purchase status provided by Google Play to determine whether Plus features are available.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Google services and SDKs:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK: ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related functions
  • Google User Messaging Platform: management of advertising consent and privacy choices
  • Google Play / Google Play Billing: app distribution, purchase processing, entitlement checks, and restoration

For more information about how these services handle information, please review the following official Google pages:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Privacy for Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android
  • Google Play Terms of Service

If the user opens an external site from reference information in the creature book or another part of the App, the privacy policy of the destination site applies.

7. Directly Identifying Information

The App currently has no feature through which the FocusDuck developer independently requests or collects the user’s name, postal address, telephone number, or email address. An optional duck name is treated as local App data.

8. Children

If a child uses the App, a parent or guardian should review this policy, the age-related information shown on Google Play, and the information provided by the relevant Google services.

9. Security

Reasonable efforts are made to manage information handled by the App appropriately for the implemented features. However, complete security cannot be guaranteed across every device, operating system, network, and third-party service.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be changed when the App’s features, third-party services, or applicable laws change. The revised policy will be published on this page.
