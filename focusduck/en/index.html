FocusDuck Grow a little duck and pond with your focus.

FocusDuck is a focus app for Android and iOS where your focused time helps a little duck and pond grow. Start a timer and your duck quietly stays by the pond with you. Each completed focus session earns breadcrumbs, while flowers and creatures gradually arrive.

No account required. Focus records and pond progress are stored on your device. FocusDuck is available on Android and iOS.
